## PREMISE

Sheikh Khalid Al-Mansoori is arriving at the hotel in 30 minutes. His assistant just called. You are the front desk supervisor. The next 14 minutes determine whether Sheikh Khalid asks for you by name on his next stay — or asks management for it.

---

## CHARACTERS

**Sheikh Khalid** — Emirati man, mid-fifties. Established family name. Speaks formal Emirati with classical register. Does not test deliberately — he notices.

**Tariq** — Jordanian, late thirties. Sheikh Khalid's personal assistant for 11 years. Handles logistics. Uses Levantine markers: كيفك, شو, بدّي, هلّأ, هون. Sheikh trusts Tariq's read of staff completely.

## SCENE 01 — Pre-Arrival Briefing (Tariq Call)

**Setting:** Front desk, 30 mins before arrival. Phone rings.

**Tariq (Jordanian):** أهلا، أنا طارق، مساعد الشيخ خالد. كيفَك؟ الشيخ بِدّو يوصَل بعد نُص ساعة، هون عَ الفُندُق. كل شي جاهِز؟

**Teaching:** Tariq's كيفَك (kifak) is Levantine; Emirati equivalent is شلونَك. بِدّو (biddo, "he wants") is Levantine; Gulf says يبغى/يبا. Asking him about Sheikh's preferences here pays off in Scene 4.

| Choice | Arabic | Romanization | T | R | C |
|--------|--------|--------------|---|---|---|
| A | أهلاً يا أبو الشيخ، الحمد لله بخير. كل شي جاهز — هل عند الشيخ تفضيلات نعرفها مسبقاً؟ | ahlan yā Abū sh-Shaykh, al-ḥamdu li-llāh bi-khayr. kull shay jāhiz — hal ʿind ish-shaykh tafḍīlāt naʿrifhā musbaqan? | +3 | +3 | +3 |
| B | أهلاً طارق، إن شاء الله كل شي جاهز | ahlan Ṭāriq, in shāʾ Allāh kull shay jāhiz | +1 | +2 | +2 |
| C | Let me check on the suite and call you back | khallīnī ashayyik wa-arujjiʿ-lak | 0 | 0 | -1 |
| D | الغرفة لسا ما جهزت — تأخير شوي | il-ġurfa lissa mā jihzat — taʾkhīr shway | -2 | -2 | -2 |

## SCENE 02 — The Arrival Greeting

**Setting:** Lobby. Sheikh Khalid walks through the door, Tariq one step behind.

**Sheikh Khalid:** السلام عليكم *(as-salāmu ʿalaykum)*

**Teaching:** Three tiers of reply to السلام عليكم. The full tier-3 is وعليكم السلام ورحمة الله وبركاته. Using يا شيخنا (with the ـنا possessive — "our sheikh") is the warm-honorific move. **Hard Rule #3: wrong honorific is worse than generic.** Calling him معالي (Minister) is incorrect — he is not a minister.

| Choice | Arabic | Romanization | T | R | C |
|--------|--------|--------------|---|---|---|
| A | وعليكم السلام ورحمة الله وبركاته — تَفَضَّل يا شيخنا، نَوَّرت | wa-ʿalaykum is-salām wa-raḥmatu llāhi wa-barakātuh — tafaḍḍal yā shaykhnā, nawwart | +3 | +3 | +3 |
| B | وعليكم السلام ورحمة الله — أهلاً يا شيخ | wa-ʿalaykum is-salām wa-raḥmatu llāh — ahlan yā shaykh | +2 | +2 | +2 |
| C ⚠️ | وعليكم السلام — أهلاً يا معالي | wa-ʿalaykum is-salām — ahlan yā maʿālī | 0 | -2 | -2 |
| D | Hello sir, welcome to the hotel | hello sir, welcome | -2 | -2 | -2 |

## SCENE 03 — The Service Request ⚠️ DIVERGENCE SCENE

**Setting:** Reception desk. Sheikh's room is ready, but he asks for an early check-in for his guests arriving at 1 PM (policy = 3 PM).

**Sheikh Khalid:** عندي ضيوف يوصَلون الساعة وحدة. الغرفة الثانية جاهزة؟

**Teaching:** Citing policy to a VIP is the documented faux pas. The cultural move is to commit, then make it happen. Note: skill rules say "policy" rebuttal to VIP is a tone failure.

| Choice | Arabic | Romanization | T | R | C |
|--------|--------|--------------|---|---|---|
| A | تَحت أمرَك يا شيخنا — ضيوفَك على الراس والعين. الغرفة بتكون جاهزة قبل وصولهم | taḥt amrak yā shaykhnā — ḍuyūfak ʿalā r-rās wal-ʿayn. il-ġurfa btikūn jāhiza qabl wuṣūlhum | +3 | +3 | +3 |
| B | إن شاء الله يا شيخ — بنرَتِّب كل شي | in shāʾ Allāh yā shaykh — binrattib kull shay | +1 | +2 | +2 |
| C ⚠️ | يا شيخ، التسجيل من الساعة ثلاث رسمياً — بس بنحاول | yā shaykh, it-tasjīl min is-sāʿa thalāth rasmiyyan — bass binḥāwil | -1 | +1 | -2 |
| D | لا يمكن قبل الساعة ثلاث | lā yumkin qabl is-sāʿa thalāth | -2 | -2 | -2 |

**⚠️ C divergence:** Respect goes up (you stated the rule, technically professional) but Trust and Culture drop (rules-citing to a VIP signals the hotel doesn't bend for him).

## SCENE 04 — Escort to Suite

**Setting:** Elevator to suite floor. Tariq mentions Sheikh prefers cold water with sliced lemon, no ice. Sheikh stays silent — Tariq is testing whether you listened in Scene 1.

**Tariq:** الشيخ بِحِبّ ماي بارد مع ليمون مُقَطَّع، بدون ثلج. هاي تفضيلاته دايماً

**Teaching:** Pre-emptive knowledge of preferences from Tariq's Scene 1 call is the cultural mastery move. If you asked about preferences in S1-A, this is your payoff.

## SCENE 05 — At the Suite Door

**Setting:** Suite door. Sheikh pauses, turns to you. Hand on his chest.

**Sheikh Khalid:** ما قصّرت يا أخي. والله ضيافة حقيقية

**Teaching:** ما قصّرت is a formal acknowledgement of full effort. The reply must match its weight. الله يخلّيك is the cultural-resonant response; هذا واجبنا (this is our duty) elevates it further.

## ENDINGS

### Exceptional — Royal Patron (نَعِم الخدمة)
**Total ≥ 42, all meters ≥ 12** — **6%**

Sheikh Khalid requests you personally on every future visit. One named VIP patron changes a hospitality career.

### Success — Glowing Review (ما شاء الله عليك)
**Total 26–41, all meters ≥ 6** — **24%**

Sheikh writes management directly. Commendation letter in your file.

### Mixed — Professional Service (مشكور)
**Total 8–25** — **48%**

Polite stay. No complaints, no compliments. Forgotten.

### Failed — Formal Complaint (الله يهديك)
**Total ≤ 7** — **22%**

Sheikh speaks to your manager. "الله يهديك" is polite disappointment.
