## PREMISE

6:45 AM. First day at a Dubai hotel. Faisal, a local colleague, greets you in the staff room. Ten minutes — over Arabic coffee you didn't expect — to become a person, not a stranger.

---

## CHARACTERS

**Faisal** — Emirati, mid-thirties. Long-serving. Warm, patient. Greets every new hire — knows which ones are worth investing in.

**Maria** — Filipina, mid-forties. Housekeeping supervisor, 8 years in Dubai. Speaks functional Arabic with Tagalog accent: skips some case endings, uses simple Gulf phrases (هلا, شكراً, تَمام, الحمد لله). Bridge character — reflects who you'll actually work with daily.

## SCENE 01 — The Greeting

**Setting:** Hotel staff room, 6:45 AM. Faisal looks up from his coffee.

**Faisal (male learner):** صباح الخير! وَيه يديد ما شفته قَبل
**Faisal (female learner):** صباح الخير! وَيه يديدة ما شفتها قَبل

**Teaching:** صباح الخير → صباح النور (not mirrored back). The mirror is the single most common beginner error.

| Choice | Arabic | Romanization | T | R | C |
|--------|--------|--------------|---|---|---|
| A | صباح النور! | ṣabāḥ in-nūr! | +3 | +3 | +3 |
| B | صباح الخير! | ṣabāḥ il-khayr! | +1 | +2 | +1 |
| C | Good morning! | — | 0 | 0 | -1 |
| D | *(nod silently)* | — | -2 | -2 | -2 |

## SCENE 02 — Introductions ⚠️ DIVERGENCE SCENE

**Setting:** Coffee machine. Faisal pours two finjans.

**Faisal (male learner):** أنا فيصل! شلونَك؟ أوّل يوم؟
**Faisal (female learner):** أنا فيصل! شلونِش؟ أوّل يوم؟

**Teaching:** Gulf "how are you" always begins with الحمد لله. تَشَرَّفنا elevates the introduction.

| Choice | Arabic | Romanization | T | R | C |
|--------|--------|--------------|---|---|---|
| A | الحمد لله، بخير! تَشَرَّفنا فيصل — أول يوم لي | al-ḥamdu li-llāh, bi-khayr! tasharrafnā Fayṣal — awwal yawm lī | +3 | +3 | +3 |
| B | الحمد لله! إي، أوّل يوم | al-ḥamdu li-llāh! ī, awwal yawm | +2 | +2 | +2 |
| C ⚠️ | إي... أوّل يوم | ī... awwal yawm | **0** | **+1** | **-1** |
| D | I'm fine, thanks. First day | — | -2 | -1 | -2 |

**⚠️ C divergence:** honest, brief, uses Khaleeji إي (Respect +1) but skips الحمد لله. Faisal notes you understood the question but missed the cultural beat.

## SCENE 03 — Maria Enters (Dialect Bridge Scene)

**Setting:** Maria walks in carrying clipboards, greets Faisal warmly, turns to you.

**Maria:** هلا! انتَ/انتي يديد/يديدة هني؟ تَمام، الحمد لله. أنا ماريا، supervisor housekeeping

**Teaching:** Real Dubai hospitality teams are majority non-Arab. Engaging Maria in Arabic — even though you could switch to English — signals respect for everyone who learned Arabic the hard way.

| Choice | Arabic | Romanization | T | R | C |
|--------|--------|--------------|---|---|---|
| A | أهلاً ماريا، تَشَرَّفنا! أوّل يوم لي — الله يَعافيش/يَعافيك | ahlan Māryā, tasharrafnā! awwal yawm lī — Allāh yʿāfīch/yʿāfīk | +3 | +3 | +3 |
| B | أهلاً ماريا، تَشَرَّفنا | ahlan Māryā, tasharrafnā | +2 | +2 | +2 |
| C | Hi Maria, nice to meet you | — | 0 | 0 | -1 |
| D | *(nod, turn back to Faisal)* | — | -2 | -2 | -2 |

## SCENE 04 — The Coffee & Farewell

**Setting:** Faisal hands you a finjan of Arabic coffee. 6:55 AM. Shift starts in 5.

**Faisal:** تَفَضَّل/تَفَضَّلي — قهوة عربية. أوّل قهوة في أوّل يوم!

**Teaching:** الله يَعافيك (m.) / يَعافيش (f.) acknowledges effort. Naming the specific kindness (the coffee, the welcome) is what people remember.

| Choice | Arabic | Romanization | T | R | C |
|--------|--------|--------------|---|---|---|
| A | الله يَعافيك يا فيصل! وايد شكراً على القهوة والترحيب | Allāh yʿāfīk yā Fayṣal! wāyid shukran ʿalā l-gahwa wat-tarḥīb | +3 | +3 | +3 |
| B | شكراً فيصل! يَلّا بالتوفيق لِكلّنا | shukran Fayṣal! yalla bit-tawfīq li-killnā | +2 | +2 | +2 |
| C | شكراً. بالتوفيق لك بَعَد | shukran. bit-tawfīq lak baʿad | +1 | +2 | +1 |
| D | *(smile, walk away)* | — | -2 | -2 | -1 |

## ENDINGS

### Exceptional — The Warm Welcome (الترحيب الحار)
**Total ≥ 28, all meters ≥ 8** — **8%**

Faisal tells the team manager you fit right in. Within a week, you have the inside info on which guests to flag, which shifts to ask for, and a standing 6:45 coffee.

### Success — The Good Start (بداية زينة)
**Total 16–27, all meters ≥ 4** — **26%**

Faisal: *"تعال بكرة نشرب قهوة"* — door open. Most new hires don't get a second-day invitation this fast.

### Mixed — The Quiet Start (بداية هادية)
**Total 4–15** — **44%**

Mostly English. Some hospitality moments declined. Faisal will say hi — won't go further.

### Failed — The Cold Start (بداية باردة)
**Total ≤ 3** — **22%**

Faisal tried. Connection didn't land. First impressions in a relationship-based industry are hard to redo.
