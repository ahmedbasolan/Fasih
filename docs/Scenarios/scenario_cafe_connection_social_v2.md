## PREMISE

A stranger, Fatima, asks if the chair next to you is free. One moment — three decisions — and the difference between a polite exchange and a family invitation next weekend.

Gulf social culture moves fast when it moves at all. The first five seconds set the tone for everything that follows.

---

## CHARACTER: Fatima

Emirati woman, late twenties. Regulars know her here. Warm, direct, quietly reads people. She will not push for connection — she creates an opening and watches what you do with it.

Speaks to a **female** learner using feminine forms: انتي, ashūfich, maʿich, ʿalaich.

## SCENE 01 — Adjacent Tables

**Setting:** Local café. You're seated. Fatima approaches your table.

**Fatima:** هذا الكُرسي فاضي؟ *(hādhā l-kursī fāḍi? — "Is this chair free?")*

**Teaching:** تَفَضَّلي is the feminine imperative. Using تَفَضَّل to a woman is grammatically wrong and noticed every time.

| Choice | Arabic | Romanization | English | Pts | Warmth | Reaction |
|--------|--------|--------------|---------|-----|--------|----------|
| A | أهلاً وسهلاً — تَفَضَّلي | ahlan wa-sahlan — tafaḍḍali | Welcome — please | +3 | +15 | She blinks, surprised. "والله ردّة فعل حلوة!" She sits, already smiling |
| B | إي تَفَضَّلي | ī tafaḍḍali | Yeah, please (sit) | +2 | +8 | Correct form. She sits, friendly but not yet engaged |
| C | آسفة، مَحجوز لِأحد | āsfa, maḥjūz li-aḥad | Sorry, it's saved for someone | 0 | -2 | Legitimate, but no opening. She thanks you and moves on → **shortened path to Scene 3 farewell** |
| D | إي *(eyes on phone)* | ī | Yeah | -2 | -12 | She sits, glances at your phone, opens her own |

→ A/B continue to **Scene 2**; C jumps to **Scene 3** (early farewell, max Mixed ending)

## SCENE 02 — Sharing the Table ⚠️ DIVERGENCE SCENE

**Setting:** Fatima has sat down. She turns toward you slightly.

**Fatima (warm — if S1=A):** انتي مِن وين؟ صراحة نادر يردّون بهالأسلوب الحلو. أول مرّة أشوفِك هني؟
**Fatima (neutral — if S1=B):** انتي مِن وين؟ أول مرّة أشوفِك هني

**Teaching:** "Where are you from?" is not a privacy probe in Gulf culture — it's the entry to your story. The strongest answer shares yourself, then asks back.

| Choice | Arabic | Romanization | English | Pts | Warmth | Reaction |
|--------|--------|--------------|---------|-----|--------|----------|
| A | أنا تَوّني يايّة هني — وانتي؟ مِن أهل المنطقة؟ | ana tawni yāya hni — w-inti? min ahl il-minṭiqa? | I just arrived here — and you? From this area? | +3 | +15 | She lights up. "إي والله، أنا مِن هني. شو رايِك في دبي؟" |
| B | أنا مِن [بلد]، تَوّني يايّة هني | ana min [country], tawni yāya hni | I'm from [country], just arrived | +2 | +8 | Friendly but no question back. She follows up to keep it going |
| C ⚠️ | ما شاء الله المكان حلو هني | mā shāʾ Allāh il-makān ḥilu hni | MashaAllah, the place is nice | **+2** | **-5** | Cultural compliment lands, but you skipped her question. Information not exchanged |
| D | أُفَضِّل ما أقول | ufaḍḍil mā aqūl | I'd rather not say | -2 | -15 | She nods slowly. "أوكي... محترَم." The opening closes |

**⚠️ C is the divergence:** technically correct Arabic compliment but deflects her question. Points for fluency, warmth lost for evasion.

→ All paths continue to **Scene 3**

## SCENE 03 — Saying Goodbye

**Setting:** Fatima checks her phone, then meets your eyes.

**Fatima (warm — if Warmth ≥ 70):** والله كان وِدّي أكَمِّل سَوالِف معِش بس لازم أروح. اِنبَسَطت وايد. نتواصَل؟
**Fatima (neutral):** كان وِدّي أكَمِّل سَوالِف بس لازم أروح. نتواصَل؟
**Fatima (cold — if Warmth < 40):** لازم أروح. يَلّا مع السَّلامة.

**Teaching:** "نتواصل؟" from someone you just met is a social risk. A hedged response ("maybe") reads as rejection. Match her weight with warmth.

| Choice | Arabic | Romanization | English | Pts | Warmth | Reaction |
|--------|--------|--------------|---------|-----|--------|----------|
| A | والله فَرحانة إني عَرَفتِش — في أمان الله | wallah farḥāna inni ʿaraftich — fī amāni l-lāh | Honestly so happy I met you — go in God's safety | +3 | +15 | She places her hand on her chest. "أنا بَعد. اِنبَسَطت" |
| B | إن شاء الله! تَشَرَّفنا يا فاطمة | in shāʾ Allāh! tasharrafnā yā Fāṭma | God willing! Honoured, Fatima | +2 | +10 | Her name + warm closing = memorable |
| C | أكيد! هذا رقمي | akīd! hādhā raqmī | Sure! Here's my number | +1 | +5 | Brief, willing. She types it in. Connection saved, warmth muted |
| D | يمكن — وايد مَشغولة هاليامين | yimkin — wāyid mashghūla hal-yāmīn | Maybe — pretty busy these days | -2 | -15 | She nods. "أوكي. الله يسهّل." She leaves. Connection closed |

## ENDINGS

### Exceptional — Lifelong Friend
**Arabic:** صديقتي العَزيزة — Points ≥ 8 AND Warmth ≥ 70 — **7%**

Fatima invites you to her family gathering next weekend. In Emirati culture, a family invitation after one café meeting is rare — it means she sees you as someone worth bringing into her inner circle.

### Success — Coffee Companion
**Arabic:** نتقابَل مرّة ثانية — Points 5–7, Warmth ≥ 55 — **23%**

You exchange numbers, plan to meet at the same café next week. Earned, not assumed.

### Mixed — Passing Acquaintance
**Arabic:** يَلّا مع السَّلامة — Points 1–4 — **48%**

Pleasant. Fatima was friendly — she always is. Friendly and connected are different things.

### Failed — Awkward Exit
**Arabic:** الله يسَهِّلِش — Points ≤ 0 — **22%**

Fatima left early. Cultural distance can feel like coldness even when none was intended.
