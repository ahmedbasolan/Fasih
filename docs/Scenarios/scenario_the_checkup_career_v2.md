## CHANGES FROM v1 — IMPLEMENT ALL

1. **Learner role specified as female nurse** — Gulf clinical reality strongly prefers female nurses for female patients. Male nurse + female elder + physical touch in v1 was culturally awkward even though clinically permitted
2. **Expanded from 4 to 5 scenes** (Intermediate requires 5–6) — added Scene 02b: privacy curtain / chaperone setup before physical exam
3. **Three meters added** (career mode mandate, was absent)
4. **Dialect contrast added**: Dr. Mostafa (Egyptian) appears in Scene 5 briefly — uses Egyptian markers
5. Numeric scoring with explicit values
6. phrasesUnlocked expanded from 8 to 12
7. All Arabic diacritized
8. Romanization on every choice
9. Divergence point added (Scene 3)
10. Community percentages added

## PREMISE

Umm Khalid is a Gulf woman, late fifties. She came to the clinic because her son told her to. She doesn't like hospitals — she told you that herself, in the first sentence she spoke.

Five small interactions. Whether she leaves feeling cared for or processed.

---

## CHARACTERS

**Umm Khalid** — Emirati woman, late fifties. Multiple adult children. Soft-spoken, perceptive. Came out of duty to her son, not trust of the system. Watching how you treat her before deciding how much to tell you.

**Dr. Mostafa** — Egyptian, late forties. Senior physician. Appears briefly in Scene 5. Uses Egyptian markers: عايز, إيه, كده.

**You** — female nurse, expat or local. All address to Umm Khalid uses feminine forms.

## SCENE 01 — Waiting Room

**Setting:** Clinic waiting area, 10 AM. Umm Khalid sits alone, holding a small purse.

**Umm Khalid:** أهلين. أنا أم خالد. كيف الحال؟

**Teaching:** خالتي is the warm honorific for older women. تَفَضَّلي (feminine imperative) must match the listener's gender.

## SCENE 02b — Privacy Setup (NEW SCENE)

**Setting:** Before BP measurement. Curtain on a rail. Umm Khalid glances at it.

**Teaching:** Verbalizing privacy options before physical exam is the cultural mastery move with female patients in Gulf clinics. Offering the curtain — even when both of you are female — signals you respect her sense of personal space.

## ENDINGS

### Exceptional — The Caring Touch (اللمسة الحنونة)
**Total ≥ 36, all meters ≥ 10** — **8%**

Umm Khalid walks out smiling and asks for you by name next time. Her son brings his wife to your clinic two weeks later.

### Success — The Good Nurse (الممرضة الزينة)
**Total 22–35, all meters ≥ 5** — **25%**

A solid visit. Umm Khalid felt respected. Comfortable, not transformed.

### Mixed — The Quiet Check (الفحص الهادي)
**Total 6–21** — **45%**

Vitals taken, questions asked. She felt processed, not cared for.

### Failed — The Cold Clinic (العيادة الباردة)
**Total ≤ 5** — **22%**

She came in saying she doesn't like clinics. You confirmed why. She'll tell her son to find another clinic — and Gulf word-of-mouth is permanent.
