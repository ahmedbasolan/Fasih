What's new vs. what stays
All existing sections are preserved exactly. The visual treatment is what changes.
Ghost Arabic letterforms
Oversized Arabic glyphs (ص، ع، ب...) sit in the background at ~3% opacity, changing per screen. They're decoration — never compete with content. Rotate slightly for organic feel.
Souk-style cards
Translucent dark surface, ultra-thin top shimmer line, subtle border. No heavy shadows. Cards breathe — feel like panels, not boxes.
Hero card with Arabic preview
Featured scenario shows the Arabic translation inside the card itself — gives the card cultural identity, not just English labels.
Eyebrow labels
Small uppercase + letterspace labels above titles (e.g. "FEATURED SCENARIO") replace section numbers and add hierarchy without clutter.
