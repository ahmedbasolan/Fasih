All your sections, order, and content stay identical. These are the visual deltas.
Ghost Arabic background
م، ح، ب bleed behind everything at ~3% opacity. Rotate slightly per screen. Invisible unless you look — but add soul.
Top shimmer line on cards
Each card has a 1px gradient line across the top edge — green fading to transparent. Replaces the heavy left-border-only look.

Uppercase labels get more breathing room and slightly lower opacity — hierarchy without shouting.
XP bar gets a glow
Progress bar uses a green→cyan gradient with a soft glow. The week-day dots get the same treatment when active.
Card surfaces
Slightly more translucent (3.8% vs your current opaque dark). Lets the ghost letters breathe through without losing readability.
